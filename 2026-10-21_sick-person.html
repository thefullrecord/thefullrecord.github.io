Trump Called It an Attack on "Free Speech"… Then Asked a Reporter "Are You a Sick Person?"

The Full Record · Oct 21, 2026

In the Oval Office on October 20, President Trump opened an event by calling a California law "an outrageous attempt to crush free speech." About an hour later he told a reporter, "Are you a sick person?" Here is everything in between, in his own words. Full video, full record: the fraud whistleblower program he will personally decide, "you better be careful," "nothing back other than high diesel," and "pretend I'm on the ballot."
