Trump Told Voters "Pretend I'm on the Ballot"… Democrats Won. His Post: "I Wasn't on the Ballot"

The Full Record · Nov 4, 2026

For weeks President Trump told rally crowds to "pretend I'm on the ballot." On Tuesday night, as Democrats took seats he had campaigned for, he posted on Truth Social: "I wasn't on the Ballot and if I was, we would have won IN A LANDSLIDE!" Seven days earlier, in Corpus Christi, Texas, he had used the same word about his own side: "we win in the landslide." This video lays his words side by side, in order: the rallies, the Speaker's predictions and the post. Results below are projections as of Wednesday morning. Michigan and Alaska were still counting.
