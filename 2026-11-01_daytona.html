Trump Promised Every Adult "$5,000" If GOP Wins… The "$2,000" Check He Promised Last Year Never Came

The Full Record · Oct 31, 2026

Four days before the midterms, President Trump told a Daytona Beach rally he would give every adult citizen $5,000 if Republicans win the House and the Senate. Last November he promised $2,000 a person. It never arrived. This is the full record of Trump's Daytona rally on Friday, October 30, 2026: the offer, where he says the money comes from, and what the tariff record shows.
