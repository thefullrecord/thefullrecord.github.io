Trump Said Roger Marshall's Bill Saves You $2,000 a Year… Then Asked "What the Hell Is It, Roger?"

The Full Record · Nov 2, 2026

Two days before the midterms, President Trump told a rally in Salina, Kansas that Sen. Roger Marshall's credit card bill would save you "at least two thousand dollars per year." Then he asked the crowd what a swipe fee is and said, "What the hell is it, Roger?" This is the full record of Trump's Sunday night rally in Salina on November 1, 2026: the promise, the numbers behind it, and the line he kept repeating, "last stop."
