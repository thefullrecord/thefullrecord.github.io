Trump Promised "At Least $2,000"... Then Target Got a $994 Million Tariff Refund

The Full Record · Oct 19, 2026

In November 2025, President Trump promised Americans a dividend "of at least $2,000." Then the Supreme Court struck down his emergency tariffs, and the refunds started going out: to the importers. Target reported $994 million.
