Trump Called Abbott "Exceptional" at 8:19 Am… 15 Hours Later: "Became a Rino." Abbott Lost

The Full Record · Nov 4, 2026

On election night, Fox News anchor Bret Baier asked President Trump on the phone whether he regretted backing Ken Paxton for the Senate in Texas. Trump answered, "Well, I had no choice." A week earlier, at a rally in Lubbock, he had called Paxton "the best ever." This video puts his words from the last two months next to the ones from Tuesday night.
