Trump Asked "On What?" About the Fired General… Then Said "She's Got Problems"

The Full Record · Oct 30, 2026

On Thursday, October 29, at Joint Base Andrews, a reporter asked President Trump about the Army general Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth had just fired. Trump asked, "On what?" Then, about the general: "She's got problems." And "we're very much looking for leakers." The general, Lieutenant General Brett Sylvia, is a man. This is the whole record, in their own words. We play the Trump gaggle first, then go back through what Hegseth himself said about secrets, about Signal and about firing generals.
