Horsford Asked Rfk Jr. About a $1,700 Premium Hike... He Said "No Cuts to Medicaid"

The Full Record · Oct 20, 2026

Rep. Steven Horsford told HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. that a constituent's marketplace premium went up $1,700 a month, and asked what he was doing about it. The first answer: "There are no cuts to Medicaid."
