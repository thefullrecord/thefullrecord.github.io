Blanche Says "Of Course Not"… Hours Later DOJ Sues to Block Alaska Senate Certification

The Full Record · Oct 24, 2026

On Thursday, asked about another election lawsuit, Attorney General Todd Blanche said: "I'm not trying to not have an election certified. Of course not." Before boarding his plane, asked about Alaska, he said it was never the department's intention to improperly interfere. Hours later, the Justice Department sued Alaska to stop it from certifying its Senate election if the Republican loses. On Friday, President Trump was asked who made the call: "I'm the one that said do it." Here is the whole week, side by side, in their own words. Sen. Dan Sullivan, a Republican, faces Democrat Mary Peltola on November 3. A second Dan Sullivan, Daniel J. Sullivan Jr., a retired teacher from Petersburg, stayed on the ballot after two courts, including the Alaska Supreme Court, put him back. President Trump called him a "disgraceful fraud," then posted that he was looking at "powerful legal options" to remove him. The next day the DOJ filed suit. We play the president's full case, then check it against the filings, and we play Blanche's strongest defense in full, including his answer on the California voter list. We also show what the senator's own campaign said, what an election law professor told PBS News, and what Trump said four seconds before "I'm the one that said do it."
