Trump Wants "Fake Dan Sullivan's" Votes Given to the "Real Dan"... His Own DOJ Said Otherwise

The Full Record · Nov 7, 2026

On November 5, President Trump said in the Oval Office that Alaska should be "mandated to take the fake Dan Sullivan's votes and the other person they put in there and give it to the real Dan, Dan Sullivan." That day the state count had Mary Peltola ahead of Sen. Dan Sullivan by 637 first-choice votes. Alaska law moves a ballot to the next choice the voter ranked. The ranked-choice count is due November 18 and certification November 25.
