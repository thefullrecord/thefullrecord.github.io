Trump Said "Zero Chance" on Wiles... 21 Days Later She's Out and Stephen Miller Has Her Job

The Full Record · Nov 7, 2026

On October 16, President Trump said there was "zero chance" Susie Wiles would be replaced as White House chief of staff and that she would be "here for a long time." Three weeks later, on November 6, he said "we got to make moves" and named Stephen Miller as the new chief of staff. Wiles moves to his political operation. Miller starts Monday, November 9.
