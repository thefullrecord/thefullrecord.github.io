Ilhan Omar Raid: Trump Said "Nothing to Do With Me"… 8 Days Later "She Could Go to Jail"

The Full Record · Oct 22, 2026

On October 12, the day federal agents searched Rep. Ilhan Omar's home, President Trump said: "That has nothing to do with me." Eight days later in the Oval Office he said: "She could also go to jail." Here is everything he said in between, side by side, in his own words. She has not been charged with a crime. The Justice Department has made no evidence public. And the president's own words, written and spoken, do not match each other.
